# Dependencies and references

The project's MIT license covers its original code, documentation, generated
models and renders. It does not relicense third-party dependencies or references.

The inspection viewer loads these dependencies from external services; they
are not embedded in the distributed HTML:

- **Three.js 0.160.0**, including its loaders and controls: MIT license.
  [Source and license](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r160).
- **Archivo** and **IBM Plex Mono**, served by Google Fonts: SIL Open Font
  License 1.1. [Archivo](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/archivo)
  and [IBM Plex Mono](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/ibmplexmono).

Blender is an external build tool and is not redistributed in this project.
Its license and notices are available in the Blender distribution.

Engineering publications are cited in `spec.py` and `docs/DESIGN_HISTORY.md`.
Their text and figures are not included in the downloadable model package.
